Risk Management Rules

This is a starting template based on common risk-management practices, not personalized financial advice — adjust the percentages to fit your own risk tolerance and account, and tell me if you want them changed.
Risk per trade — 1% of account. At — equity, that's about — max loss (from entry to stop) on any single trade.
Max daily loss — 3% of account. About —. Hit it → stop trading for the day, no exceptions.
Max weekly loss — 6% of account. About —. Hit it → cut size in half until a green week resets it.
Position sizing: shares = (account × 1%) ÷ (entry price − stop price). Round down. Never size up to "make it back."
Prioritize A+, A-, B+ setups (trend-aligned — EMA clouds over the 34/50 for longs, under for shorts). B-, C, D setups are counter-trend and lower probability — take them smaller, or skip them, per the Tenet playbook.
Journal every trade the same day it closes — ticker, setup, what worked or didn't. Review the log weekly: cut what's not working, size up what is.
